SVG Image Fixer

Experience Design Solutions · Figma export fixer

Keep your images when you open SVGs in Illustrator.

Some Figma SVG exports lose their images in Illustrator and similar tools. Add one or many files and get fixed copies where the images show. Files are processed on this computer and never uploaded.

Works with images placed inside a shape through a pattern, the way Figma exports them. Rectangles, paths and single-shape masks are supported. Anything else is left as it was and listed under the file.
